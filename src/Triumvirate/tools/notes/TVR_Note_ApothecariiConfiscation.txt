<b>League of the Apothecarii</b>

<b>Record of Confiscation</b>


Concerning: Count Samael Dal'Galar, formerly Apothecarius of this League


By decision of the League, the following were seized from the Count's wagons at the city gate on the morning he departed Ark for the Frostcliff Mountains, and are to be kept in this chest under lock and key:


- Five spell tomes in the Count's own hand, by which a raven, a nest of rattlesnakes, a gray wolf, a snow leopard and a glacier hound may be called out of the Sea of Eventualities and bound to the caster's will.

- Five spell tomes of a cruder, older make, for the raising of totems of water, tree and earth, and for the calling of the wind and sun spirits the old shamans named Fylgjas.

- Three staves bearing the same totem workings.


The Count protested that these were "instruments of healing" and that the League had no right to them. Entropy is no instrument of healing. Those of us who saw his workshop will not soon forget it. The Healer of the Poor may treat whomever he pleases in his mountains. He will not do it with these.


A further note for whoever keeps this chest after me: The blueprint for the Old Ban Totem is to stay here with the tomes, and is not to leave this house. It is a Psionic working, and therefore as forbidden as the rest - but it is also the only thing we know of that breaks the warding on the creature in the Tar Pit, the one the miners call the Blind Miner. Steel and spell glance off him. Within reach of the totem's ban, they do not. Should the Order ever have need of it, send them to the League, and to no one else.


- Keeper of Records, House of the Apothecarii
